Features

Everything a client project needs, nothing it doesn't

Harbor covers the boring middle of client work — the part that usually lives in six tabs and a shared spreadsheet.

Branded client portals

Every client gets a link like harbor.to/yourstudio with your logo and colours. Remove Harbor branding on any paid plan.

Proposals and e-signatures

Send the scope, collect a legally binding signature, and convert it into a live project without retyping a single line item.

Files that stay organized

Drop the final deliverables in a folder and clients get versioned downloads, plus a record of who grabbed what and when.

Invoices that chase themselves

Polite reminders go out at 3, 7 and 14 days. Card and bank transfer are built in, and late fees are configured once and forgotten.

Time tracking to payment

Log time against a task, convert the block into a line item, and add it to the next invoice in two clicks. No exports required.

Approvals with context

Clients approve a milestone or leave a comment right on the deliverable, so feedback stops vanishing into a reply-all thread.

FAQ

Questions we get before sign-up

If yours isn't here, write to hello@example.com — a person answers, usually within a few hours.

Do my clients need to create an account?

No. Clients open the link, enter the one-time code we email them, and they're in. If you'd rather they set a password, you can switch that on per client.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. On Studio and Agency plans you can serve portals from portal.yourstudio.com. You add a CNAME record and we handle the certificate — there's no renewal to remember.

What happens when the free trial ends?

The trial runs 14 days with no card required. Afterwards you can pick a plan or stay on the free tier for up to three clients. Nothing is deleted, paused or held hostage if you wait a few weeks to decide.

Can I import my existing clients and invoices?

Yes — CSV import covers clients, projects and unpaid invoices, and you can preview every row before anything is created. Agency plans add a two-way sync with QuickBooks and Xero.

How do you handle data protection and GDPR?

Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in the EU or US region you pick at sign-up. We sign DPAs, keep sub-processors listed publicly, offer full data export at any time, and delete accounts within 30 days of a written request.
