# Master Shoot Script — 7-Second and 15-Second Vertical Promo

Shoot-ready scripts for a 9:16 product/promo short. Every beat below tells you exactly
what to record, what words appear on screen, what the voiceover says, and where the
cut lands. Nothing here needs to be written — only filmed.

**Format:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16), 30 fps, H.264/MP4.
**Frame math:** at 30 fps, 1 second = 30 frames. "f120" = frame 120 = 0:04.0.

**Example product used throughout this file:** *LedgerLite* — a $29 personal-finance
dashboard. Swap the tokens below for your product and every line still works:

| Token | Example value | Appears in |
|---|---|---|
| `[PRODUCT]` | LedgerLite | on-screen text, VO, end card |
| `[WHAT]` | finance dashboard | VO, description text |
| `[OUTCOME]` | know where every dollar goes | hook text, VO |
| `[PROOF]` | 4.9★ from 300+ buyers | proof beat (replace with your real numbers) |
| `[PRICE]` | $29 one-time | end card (delete if free) |
| `[CTA]` | Link in bio | CTA beat (or "DM me the word START", "Tap the cart") |

## C. TEXT-OVERLAY SPEC (applies to both versions)

Canvas: **1080 × 1920**. All coordinates are pixels from the top-left corner.

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Main headline | Bold condensed sans (Anton, Archivo Black, Bebas Neue, or CapCut "Bebas" preset). **72 px** cap height, ALL CAPS, letter-spacing −2%. |
| Secondary line | Same family, Semi-Bold, **56 px**, ALL CAPS. |
| Eyebrow / label | **40 px**, Medium, ALL CAPS, letter-spacing +8%, 80% opacity. |
| Color | Pure white `#FFFFFF` with a **6 px black stroke** OR a black `#0A0A0A` box behind the text. Never both. Never text on a busy background without one of them. |
| Position | Top third: text block sits between **y = 300 px and y = 720 px**. Center: **y = 810–1110 px**. Never below **y = 1480 px** (platform caption zone). Never above **y = 250 px**. |
| Safe margins | Keep everything inside **x = 90–990 px** (left) so the right-side action rail never covers it. |
| Entrance | Pop-in: scale 96% → 100%, opacity 0 → 100%, **120 ms, ease-out**. No fade longer than 150 ms — it reads as slow. |
| Hold | Text that carries meaning holds **minimum 0.8 s**; text under 8 characters may hold 0.5 s. |
| Exit | Hard cut with the shot, or 100 ms fade. Never animate text out on its own. |
| Lines | Max **2 lines**, max **26 characters per line**. If it doesn't fit, cut words — don't shrink the font. |
| Caption offset | Platform captions (auto-captions) must start at **y ≥ 1500 px** so they never collide with your overlay. |

## D. SOUND & MUSIC NOTE

- **Track style:** instrumental only, 110–125 BPM, one clear drop/impact per 2 seconds so
  cuts have somewhere to land. Two safe moods: (a) punchy trap-lite hi-hat groove for
  product/tech, (b) warm lo-fi keys for lifestyle/service.
- **Ducking:** music at −6 dB while the VO plays, full level in gaps. VO peaks around
  −12 dBFS average / −3 dBFS max.
- **SFX you need (with timecodes for the 15s version):** whoosh f0, whoosh f45,
  paper slide f96/f126/f156, UI click f241/f264, star pop f306, bell f348, impact f390.
  The 7s version needs only: whoosh f0, UI tap f90, ticks f141/f147, impact f180.
- **Ending:** leave the last 0.2 s of music ringing rather than fading to silence —
  hard silence at the end is what makes a loop sound broken.
- **Never** let music with vocals sit under a voiceover; the VO will fight it.

## E. WHAT YOU RECORD (7 clips, ~6 minutes of shooting)

1. Spreadsheet screen capture, scrolling (10 s) — hook A
2. Receipts dropped on desk, overhead (10 s) — hook B / problem beat
3. Over-the-shoulder dashboard swipe (15 s) — demo
4. Locked-off screen recording, dashboard + tap (20 s) — demo
5. Macro of rating/reviews screen with slow rotation (15 s) — proof
6. Hero shot: product on desk, key light at 45° (15 s) — CTA
7. Brand-color end card (made as a still in Canva, 1 s in the edit)

Record everything at 1080p or higher, **30 fps**, phone locked where possible.
Shoot the hero and proof beats twice — once normal, once 6% closer. You will always
use the closer one.
