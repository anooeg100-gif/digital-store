# 5 Complete, Ready-to-Shoot Promo Scripts

Five finished scripts for five different niches. Each one is written out to the exact
word: on-screen text, voiceover, shot direction, cut points, and sound. Film it as-is,
or swap the example product for yours — the structure and timing carry over unchanged.

All scripts: **9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps.** Frames shown at 30 fps.

## Quick adaptation rules (all five scripts)

1. Keep the beat structure; replace only the text and VO columns.
2. Re-record proof numbers with *your* real numbers — never keep someone else's.
3. If your VO runs long, cut from the middle beat, never the hook or the last line.
4. One CTA per video. Two CTAs means the viewer takes neither.
