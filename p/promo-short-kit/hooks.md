# 20 Hook Formulas for 7–15 Second Promos

Every hook below = a fill-in formula + a finished example you can shoot today.
Rules for all 20: say it in the first 1.0 seconds, put it on screen as text (silent
viewers are the majority), and never spend a hook on your brand name.

---

## CURIOSITY — open a loop the viewer needs closed

**1. The unfinished sentence**
> Formula: `[SHOCKING STATEMENT]... and that's why [SURPRISING CONSEQUENCE]`
> Example: **"I deleted half my landing page… and doubled my signups."**

**2. The forbidden number**
> Formula: `The [ODD NUMBER] [THINGS/NAMES/RULES] nobody tells you about [TOPIC]`
> Example: **"The 3 pricing mistakes that quietly kill SaaS trials."**

**3. The withheld reveal**
> Formula: `[RELATABLE RESULT] — [PRODUCT] is the reason, and I'm not gatekeeping it`
> Example: **"My whole desk setup runs on one $19 file. Here it is."**

**4. The reverse-engineered question**
> Formula: `Why does [SOMETHING OBVIOUS] always [WEIRD BEHAVIOR]?`
> Example: **"Why does your best idea always arrive in the shower?"**

---

## PAIN — name the frustration before they scroll

**5. The mirror**
> Formula: `If your [DAILY TASK] takes longer than [SHORT TIME], [PRODUCT] is for you`
> Example: **"If your invoice follow-ups take longer than 4 seconds, read this."**

**6. The last-straw confession**
> Formula: `I [EMBARRASSING THING YOU KEPT DOING]. Then I [SWITCH].`
> Example: **"I was still copy-pasting captions by hand. Then I built a swipe file."**

**7. The double cost**
> Formula: `[PAIN TASK] isn't costing you [SMALL THING]. It's costing you [BIG THING].`
> Example: **"Chasing revisions isn't costing you hours. It's costing you the client."**

**8. The quit-list**
> Formula: `Three things I stopped doing to [DESIRED OUTCOME] — #2 hurt most`
> Example: **"Three things I stopped doing to sleep before midnight. Number two hurt."**

---

## PROOF — lead with evidence, not adjectives

**9. Receipts-first**
> Formula: `[NUMBER] [BUYERS/SALES/USERS] in [TIME PERIOD]. Here's what they bought.`
> Example: **"142 decks sold in 9 days — here's the exact file they got."**

**10. The screen-record confession**
> Formula: `This is my [REAL SCREEN], not a mockup. Watch what happens at [MOMENT].`
> Example: **"This is my actual analytics. Watch what happens on day 6."**

**11. The third-party line**
> Formula: `[REAL QUOTE OR REVIEW] — I didn't write that, my customer did.`
> Example: **"'I stopped dreading Mondays.' — a review I got at 1:14 a.m."**

---

## CONTRAST — before/after is the oldest cut in the video

**12. The hard split**
> Formula: `[OLD WAY] vs. [NEW WAY]. Same task, [TIME DIFFERENCE].`
> Example: **"Hour-long edit vs. 9-minute edit. Same video, same footage."**

**13. The version upgrade**
> Formula: `Me a year ago: [BAD HABIT]. Me now: [HABIT] — same person.`
> Example: **"Me a year ago: 14 tabs open. Me now: one dashboard. Same workload."**

**14. The honest downgrade**
> Formula: `I made [X] worse on purpose — [BETTER RESULT] came anyway`
> Example: **" I made my offer worse on purpose. Sales went up."**

---

## URGENCY — a real reason to act now (never fake scarcity)

**15. The deadline that exists**
> Formula: `This price/offer changes on [REAL DATE]. Not manufactured — [REASON].`
> Example: **"The launch price ends Friday because the bonus cohort closes Friday."**

**16. The cost of waiting**
> Formula: `Every [DAY/WEEK] you wait, [SPECIFIC LOSS] adds up.`
> Example: **"Every week you delay your tax filing, the late fee climbs."**

**17. The seasonal fit**
> Formula: `[REAL SEASON/EVENT] is [TIME AWAY]. This is the [PREP THING] for it.`
> Example: **"Tax season is 6 weeks out. This is the folder that makes it a 1-hour job."**

---

## IDENTITY — people buy who they want to be

**18. The in-group test**
> Formula: `If you're the friend who [IDENTITY BEHAVIOR], you need [PRODUCT].`
> Example: **"If you're the friend who reorganizes the shared calendar, you need this."**

**19. The quiet-flex**
> Formula: `Nobody [VISIBLE WIN]. They just [BORING THING] for [TIME].`
> Example: **"Nobody 'gets organized.' They just spend 12 minutes on Sunday. Every week."**

**20. The anti-audience callout**
> Formula: `[PRODUCT] isn't for you if [HONEST DISQUALIFIER] — it's for people who [WHO IT'S FOR].`
> Example: **"This isn't for people who want more templates. It's for people who hate starting from zero."**

---

### Choosing one in 10 seconds

- **Selling to cold traffic:** curiosity (1–4) or contrast (12–14) — they don't know
  your category yet, so lead with tension, not features.
- **Selling to people who clicked your profile:** proof (9–11) — they already know you.
- **Retargeting / cart abandoners:** pain (5–8) or urgency (15–17), with a real deadline only.
- **Community / audience building:** identity (18–20) — the comment section is the conversion.
