# Caption Packs for the 5 Scripts

Copy-paste captions for every script in `scripts.md`. Three variants each:

- **Short** — for when the video does the talking (pinned promos, ad-style posts)
- **Storytelling** — for feed posts where the caption carries the context
- **CTA** — for launch days, sales, and profile promos

Hashtags are chosen to mix one broad tag, two mid-size niche tags, and one branded tag
you replace with your own. Never paste all three captions — pick one per post.
