# Storyboard Template

Fill this table before you shoot and the edit becomes assembly, not decisions.
Two boards below are pre-filled from `scripts.md`; copy the blank one for your own.

Columns: **Frame #** (start frame, 30 fps) · **Time** · **Visual** (what the lens sees)
· **On-screen text** (exact words) · **Audio** (VO + music + SFX) · **Transition**
(how we get out of the shot).

- [ ] No text below y = 1480 px or above y = 250 px

- [ ] VO for each beat fits the beat length at ~3 words/second
