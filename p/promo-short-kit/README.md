# Promo Short Kit

A shoot-ready kit for making 7-second and 15-second vertical promos for any product,
service, or profile. You don't write anything — you pick a script, film what it says,
and upload. Built for 9:16 (TikTok, Reels, Shorts, X).

---

## What's inside

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `video/script.md` | Master shoot script: full 7-second and 15-second versions, beat by beat, with exact on-screen text, voiceover, frames, cut direction, sound cues, text-overlay spec, and the 7 clips you record |
| `hooks.md` | 20 hook formulas grouped by persuasion type (curiosity, pain, proof, contrast, urgency, identity) — each with the formula and a finished example |
| `scripts.md` | 5 complete ready-to-shoot scripts for 5 niches: SaaS tool (15 s), design template pack (7 s), coaching/service (15 s), physical product (7 s), creator/personal brand (7 s) — each with an alternate CTA variant |
| `storyboard.md` | Storyboard table (frame / time / visual / text / audio / transition), pre-filled for two of the scripts, plus a blank copy with a pre-shoot checklist |
| `captions.md` | For each of the 5 scripts: a short, a storytelling, and a CTA caption with hashtags, plus a hashtag bank sorted by niche |
| `specs.md` | Export preset, per-platform specs (exact pixels, bitrate, fps, duration and file limits), safe zones for TikTok/Reels/Shorts/X, file naming convention, and a pre-upload checklist |
| `video/cover.png`, `video/promo.mp4`, `video/promo.gif` | Preview media for this kit |

## The 10-minute workflow

**0:00–02:00 — Pick.** Open `scripts.md`, choose the script closest to your product.
If none match, take the structure of any script and swap in your words, or start from
`video/script.md` and replace the tokens (`[PRODUCT]`, `[OUTCOME]`, `[PROOF]`,
`[PRICE]`, `[CTA]`).

**02:00–07:00 — Shoot.** Film the clips listed in section E of `video/script.md` —
a phone propped against a stack of books is enough. Shoot each beat twice; use the
tighter take. Record your voiceover on the phone in one take per beat, phone 20 cm
from your mouth, quiet room.

**07:00–08:30 — Caption.** Assemble in CapCut (or your editor): drop clips on the
beat times in the script, paste the on-screen text from the table, lay the VO, duck
the music. Pick one caption from `captions.md`.

**08:30–10:00 — Upload.** Run the checklist in `specs.md`, export with the master
preset, post through the platform's own upload, paste the caption, check the link
in bio. Done.

## Adapting a script to your own product

1. **Replace only two columns.** On-screen text and voiceover are product-specific;
   timing, shots, cuts, and sound are already solved — keep them.
2. **Re-do the proof beat with your real numbers.** Never keep a sample number.
   No numbers yet? Use the refund/guarantee beat or an honest identity line instead.
3. **Match the length to the job.** 7 s for anything you'll run as an ad or pin;
   15 s when you need to show the product working.
4. **Re-time the VO.** Speech runs about 3 words per second. If a line overruns its
   beat, cut words from the middle of the line — never from the hook.
5. **One CTA.** Each script ships with a variant CTA for a second post; use one per
   video, and make sure the link it names actually exists.
6. **Hook first.** If your first 2 seconds don't contain a full sentence of text and
   a complete spoken line, the rest won't get watched.

## File and format notes

- **Everything is plain Markdown (UTF-8)** — opens in any editor, phone, or notes app.
- **Timings are in frames at 30 fps.** f180 = 0:06.0. At 60 fps, double the frame numbers.
- **Canvas for all text specs: 1080 × 1920 (9:16).** Sizes are in pixels on that canvas.
- **Video deliverables:** MP4, H.264, AAC audio, 30 fps, 10–16 Mbps — the full preset
  is in `specs.md`.
- The sample products (LedgerLite, ReplyFox, Bolddeck, Offer Lab, Corkstand,
  @mayabuilds.budget) are worked examples so you can see the scripts finished — swap
  them for your own before you post.
- Claims: keep every number you put on screen true and verifiable. Promos with honest
  proof outperform promos with invented proof the moment anyone checks.
