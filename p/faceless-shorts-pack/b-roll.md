# B-Roll Shot List + Legally Free Footage Sources

**Part 1** is a shot library: 36 concrete, specific shots — write the shot ID on your
shoot list or search the stock sites for the description in quotes. **Part 2** is where
to get footage for free and exactly what the licenses allow.

Every shot below is written for **9:16 vertical** — most phone-shot clips are natively
vertical already; for stock, filter to "portrait" before you search.

### Shooting rules that make stock match your footage
- **One grade per video.** Warm OR cool — mixing stock temperatures is the tell.
- **Move the camera or the light, not both.** Every clip should have exactly one motion.
- **3–5 seconds minimum per clip** so you can cut on the beat and still have handles.
- **Shoot your own hands and spaces** for proof beats; use stock for mood and filler only.
- **No faces without a release.** Stock licenses cover the clip, but if a recognizable
  person is the subject of YOUR original footage, get written permission.

## Part 2 — Where to get footage legally (free)

All URLs are the real libraries; licenses can change, so re-check the license page on
the day you download — and always check the license shown on the individual asset.

| Source | License (what it allows) | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **Pexels** (pexels.com/license) | Pexels License: free commercial and non-commercial use, modification allowed, **no attribution required** (they appreciate credit). | Can't sell unaltered copies, can't imply the people in a photo endorse you, can't redistribute on a stock platform. |
| **Pixabay** (pixabay.com/service/license-summary) | Pixabay Content License: free commercial use, **no attribution required**, modification allowed. | Some items (esp. music and logo-like content) carry extra restrictions shown on the asset page — read it. |
| **Mixkit** (mixkit.co/license) | Mixkit Free License: free for commercial and non-commercial video/music/SFX projects, **no attribution required**. | Can't't redistribute the assets as stock footage themselves (e.g., re-upload to a stock site). |
| **Coverr** (coverr.co/license) | Coverr License: free commercial use in videos and ads, **no attribution required**. | Same rule: no reselling the raw clips. |
| **Videvo** (videvo.net/license) | Mixed: many clips are **Videvo Free License — attribution required** (copy the credit line into your description); others are CC0; premium clips under a paid license. | Filter by license before you download — the free-vs-CC0-vs-premium mix is easy to misread. |
| **Unsplash** (unsplash.com/license) | Unsplash License: free to use and modify, commercial and non-commercial, attribution not required (appreciated). | Mostly photos (great for Ken Burns pans); no selling unaltered copies, no building a competing collection. |
| **Wikimedia Commons** (commons.wikimedia.org) | **License varies per file** — CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, or public domain. The license is on each file page. | CC BY ⇒ you must credit; CC BY-SA ⇒ your video may need the same license. Check every file, every time. |
| **NASA image/video library** (images.nasa.gov) | Generally public domain / free for use under NASA media usage guidelines. | NASA insignia and identifiable people have separate restrictions; some third-party content in NASA items isn't public domain. |
| **Internet Archive** (archive.org) | **Item-specific** — each item has a Rights page. Many films are public domain; many aren't. | Never assume: open the item, read the rights field, note it for your records. |
| **YouTube Audio Library** (in YouTube Studio) | Free music and SFX for use in videos, including monetized ones. | Some tracks require attribution (labeled on the track) — copy the credit into your description. Don't pull the audio out and redistribute it. |
| **Your own screen recordings & phone clips** | Yours, automatically. | Blur account names, notifications, other people's messages, and license plates before posting. |

### Music & sound — the honest rules
1. **In-app music libraries (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) are licensed for use inside that app.**
   Downloading the audio and re-using it in an ad, a product video, or another platform
   is outside that license. Business/ads accounts should use the platform's Commercial
   Music Library or a licensed library instead.
2. **Credit is not a license.** "Credit to the owner" in a description does not grant
   you rights to someone else's track, image, or clip.
3. **Royalty-free is not free-for-everything.** Read whether the license covers ads,
   client work, and monetized video — most reputable libraries do, but confirm.
4. **AI-generated "stock" clips** may have unclear training/provenance — for client
   work, prefer sources with a stated license over anything scraped from a social feed.
5. **When in doubt, film it.** A 10-second shot of your own desk costs nothing and
   can never be taken down.
