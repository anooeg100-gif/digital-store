# Tech / AI Short — "You're Prompting Wrong: The 4-Line Frame"
**Format:** screen-record tutorial · **Duration:** ~52 s · **Narration:** ~135 words ≈ 2.6 words/sec

## Hook (0:00–0:04)
- **On-screen text:** `0:00.3` → `YOUR PROMPT IS MISSING 3 LINES` (64 px, top third, pop-in)
- **Narration:** "You're prompting the AI wrong — and it's not the model's fault."
- **Visual:** Screen recording, real speed: a vague prompt is typed ("write me something about coffee"), and the generic mush answer scrolls past. Push in 6% on the answer.
- **Transition:** Hard cut on "fault."

## Scene 2 — The diagnosis (0:04–0:14)
- **On-screen text:** `0:04.5` → `VAGUE IN → MUSH OUT` (64 px) · `0:09` → `THE 4-LINE FRAME` (64 px) · `0:11.5` → `ROLE · TASK · FORMAT · EXAMPLE` (48 px, four words popping one by one)
- **Narration:** "Vague input gets you an average answer, because the model has nothing specific to work with. The fix isn't a smarter prompt — it's a frame: who the AI is, what you want, how it should come out, and what good looks like."
- **Visual:** The messy answer gets crossed out with a red line (drawn in-editor), then the four words appear over a plain dark background, one per beat. No footage — this scene is pure text.
- **Transition:** Push-through.

## Scene 3 — The frame in action (0:14–0:28)
- **On-screen text:** the four prompt lines appear as typed text, one per second: `YOU ARE A COPYWRITER FOR A COFFEE SUBSCRIPTION` (0:14.5) · `WRITE A 90-WORD WELCOME EMAIL` (0:18.5) · `3 SENTENCES. FRIENDLY. NO EMOJI.` (0:22.5) · `TONE: "YOUR FIRST BAG SHIPS TUESDAY."` (0:25.5) — all 44 px, monospace font, left-aligned like a real editor
- **Narration:** "Watch. You are a copywriter for a coffee subscription. Write a ninety-word welcome email. Three sentences, friendly, no emoji. Tone example: your first bag ships Tuesday. Then hit enter."
- **Visual:** Continuous screen recording of those exact four lines being typed (speed up the typing 2×, slow the read back to normal), then the generated result appearing. Keep the result on screen at least 1.5 seconds — viewers will pause it.
- **Transition:** Hard cut after the result lands.

## Scene 4 — The fix loop (0:28–0:39)
- **On-screen text:** `0:28.5` → `WRONG ANSWER? FIX ONE LINE` (48 px, two lines) · `0:33.5` → `NOT THE WHOLE PROMPT` (56 px) · `0:37` → `SPECIFICITY = CONTROL` (48 px)
- **Narration:** "When the answer's off, don't rewrite the whole thing — change one line. Too formal? Fix the tone line. Wrong length? Fix the format line. You're steering, not restarting. Specificity is the only control panel you get."
- **Visual:** Screen recording: cursor highlights only the tone line, retypes it, regenerates — one clean take. Add a small arrow annotation pointing at the edited line.
- **Transition:** 6-frame white flash.

## Scene 5 — Why this beats tricks (0:39–0:47)
- **On-screen text:** `0:39.5` → `NO SECRET WORDS. NO "JAILBREAKS."` (44 px, two lines) · `0:44` → `JUST CONTEXT` (64 px)
- **Narration:** "No secret words, no magic phrases, no forty-page prompt libraries. The models only ever work with what you actually gave them. Give them four clean lines and most of the 'AI is dumb' moments disappear."
- **Visual:** Static shot of a chat window with the four-line prompt visible at the top, slow zoom out — let the viewer re-read it. Grade slightly cooler than the rest.
- **Transition:** Hard cut.

## CTA (0:47–0:53)
- **On-screen text:** `0:47.5` → `PRACTICAL AI. NO HYPE.` (56 px) · `0:49.5` → `FOLLOW` (80 px, pill, hold to end)
- **Narration:** "Follow for practical AI — no hype, no affiliate links in the fine print."
- **Visual:** End card, near-black background, handle centered, hold final 8 frames.
- **Transition:** None — hold to end.

### Production notes
- This one lives or dies on screen-recording legibility: capture at 1080×1920 (not a horizontal window cropped in post), browser zoom at 120%+, and never show personal tabs or accounts.
- Layout: monospace text at 44 px for prompt lines (see **Boxed Keyword** in `templates.md` for the alternating beats).
- Music: minimal electronic, low; the typing is the rhythm — sync the kick to line completions.
- If you re-shoot this for your own tool, swap the example prompt for one from YOUR workflow; generic examples read as untested.
