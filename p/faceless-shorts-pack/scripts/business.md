# Business Short — "Sell It Before You Build It"
**Format:** contrarian rule · **Duration:** ~53 s · **Narration:** ~135 words ≈ 2.6 words/sec

## Hook (0:00–0:04)
- **On-screen text:** `0:00.3` → `DON'T BUILD IT YET` (72 px, top third, pop-in) · `0:02` → `SELL IT FIRST` (72 px, red accent)
- **Narration:** "Don't build it yet. Sell it first — before the first line of code, the first batch, the first lesson."
- **Visual:** A product box, mockup, or prototype sitting half-unwrapped on a table, then a hand slides it back off-frame. One continuous take, overhead, locked off.
- **Transition:** Hard cut on "lesson."

## Scene 2 — Why products fail (0:04–0:14)
- **On-screen text:** `0:04.5` → `MOST FAILURES AREN'T BUILT WRONG` (44 px, two lines) · `0:09` → `THEY'RE BUILT FOR NOBODY` (56 px)
- **Narration:** "Most failed products weren't built badly. They were built for nobody — for an idea of a customer nobody actually checked. Building is the expensive part. Believing is the free part, and that's where the damage starts."
- **Visual:** Empty warehouse shelf or a stack of unsold boxes in a storage unit, one slow lateral move. Desaturated grade. Stock footage works fine here — see `b-roll.md`.
- **Transition:** Push-through.

## Scene 3 — The test (0:14–0:27)
- **On-screen text:** `0:14.5` → `RULE FROM "THE MOM TEST"` (44 px, top) · `0:18.5` → `❌ "WOULD YOU BUY THIS?"` (56 px, struck through) · `0:22.5` → `✅ "HOW DO YOU HANDLE THIS TODAY?"` (56 px, green accent)
- **Narration:** "Rob Fitzpatrick's rule: never ask if someone would buy. People say yes to be kind, and you'll build a business on compliments. Instead ask how they deal with the problem today. If the answer is 'we don't — and it's fine'… you just saved six months."
- **Visual:** Text-led scene over a desk two-shot: two coffee cups, one person's hands gesturing in explanation, shot from behind so no face shows. Each text beat lands on a gesture.
- **Transition:** Hard cut.

## Scene 4 — The only real vote (0:27–0:38)
- **On-screen text:** `0:27.5` → `OPINIONS ARE FREE` (56 px) · `0:31` → `DEPOSITS AREN'T` (56 px) · `0:34.5` → `PRE-ORDER · WAITLIST WITH A PRICE · SIGNED COMMITMENT` (40 px, two lines)
- **Narration:** "The only vote that costs somebody something is money. A pre-order, a deposit, a waitlist with a price attached. Ten real conversations with that kind of answer beat a thousand survey clicks."
- **Visual:** Close-up of a payment confirmation on a phone (use your own screen, blur any real amounts and names), then a quick cut to a notebook with ten tally marks drawn in. Warm light returns.
- **Transition:** 6-frame white flash.

## Scene 5 — The honest part (0:38–0:47)
- **On-screen text:** `0:38.5` → `SOMETIMES YOU'LL BUILD ON HOPE` (44 px, two lines) · `0:42.5` → `THAT'S A HOPE, NOT A BUSINESS` (44 px, two lines, plain white on black)
- **Narration:** "Sometimes you'll hear nothing and build anyway — curiosity is allowed. Just know what you have: a hope, not a business, until somebody pays for it. Track that distinction honestly and you'll never be surprised by your own launch."
- **Visual:** Static shot of an empty notebook page with the word "HOPE" written at the top — nothing else. Locked off, two full seconds.
- **Transition:** Hard cut.

## CTA (0:47–0:53)
