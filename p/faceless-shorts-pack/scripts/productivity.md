# Productivity Short — "Stop Deciding at 9 a.m."
**Format:** system/how-to · **Duration:** ~53 s · **Narration:** ~135 words ≈ 2.6 words/sec

## Hook (0:00–0:04)
- **On-screen text:** `0:00.3` → `STOP DECIDING AT 9 A.M.` (72 px, top third, pop-in)
- **Narration:** "Stop making decisions at nine in the morning. That's where the day dies."
- **Visual:** Alarm clock flipping to 9:00 in a dark room, one slow push-in. Filmed straight on, shallow depth of field on the numbers.
- **Transition:** Hard cut on "dies."

## Scene 2 — The real problem (0:04–0:15)
- **On-screen text:** `0:04.5` → `WHAT FIRST? · WHAT ORDER? · IS THIS EVEN IT?` (48 px, three lines popping left to right)
- **Narration:** "Every morning you wake up and start negotiating with yourself: what first, what order, is this even the right task. That negotiation — not the work — is the exhaustion you feel by ten."
- **Visual:** Desk overhead: hand hovers between laptop, notebook, and phone, moves three times, touches nothing. Slightly sped up (1.15×) so the indecision reads as restless.
- **Transition:** Hard cut.

## Scene 3 — The fix (0:15–0:25)
- **On-screen text:** `0:15.5` → `5 MINUTES. THE NIGHT BEFORE.` (56 px) · `0:19.5` → `ONE MAIN TASK + TWO SUPPORTERS` (56 px) · `0:23` → `NOT A LIST — A DECISION` (48 px)
- **Narration:** "The fix takes five minutes the night before. Write tomorrow's one main task — the one that makes everything else easier — plus two supporting tasks. That's it. Not a list: a decision."
- **Visual:** Warm lamp light, hand writing exactly three lines in a notebook. Camera at 30° from above, tight on the pen. Write it in real time — do not speed this up.
- **Transition:** 6-frame whip-pan.

## Scene 4 — The payoff (0:25–0:34)
- **On-screen text:** `0:25.5` → `9:00 A.M. → JUST START` (64 px)
- **Narration:** "When you sit down the next morning, there's nothing left to decide. You just start. Starting was never the hard part — choosing was."
- **Visual:** Same desk, morning daylight, laptop opening in one motion and hands landing on the keyboard. Match the framing of Scene 2 so the contrast is visible without explanation.
- **Transition:** Hard cut.

## Scene 5 — The upgrade (0:34–0:44)
- **On-screen text:** `0:34.5` → `TIME-BOX IT: 90 MINUTES` (56 px) · `0:38.5` → `PHONE IN ANOTHER ROOM` (56 px) · `0:42` → `NO CLOCK-WATCHING` (44 px)
- **Narration:** "Give each task a time box: ninety minutes, phone in another room. You can do anything for ninety minutes — and you can't multitask your way out of a single block."
- **Visual:** Three cuts: (a) kitchen timer set to 90:00, (b) phone dropped into a drawer and shut, (c) clock face or timer running with the second hand moving. One second each.
- **Transition:** Hard cut per beat.

## Scene 6 — The closer (0:44–0:49)
- **On-screen text:** `0:44.5` → `5 TONIGHT → 1 HOUR SAVED TOMORROW` (56 px, two lines, centered)
- **Narration:** "Five minutes tonight buys back the first hour tomorrow. That's the whole system."
- **Visual:** Notebook closing on the desk under lamp light — a physical "done" gesture. Locked off.
- **Transition:** Hard cut.

## CTA (0:49–0:55)
- **On-screen text:** `0:49.5` → `ONE SYSTEM AT A TIME` (56 px) · `0:51.5` → `FOLLOW` (80 px, pill, hold to end)
- **Narration:** "Follow — one working system at a time."
- **Visual:** End card, warm dark background, handle centered, hold final 8 frames for a clean loop.
- **Transition:** None — hold to end.

### Production notes
- The whole short runs on **matched framing**: Scene 2 (messy morning) and Scene 4 (clean morning) must be shot from the identical tripod position — that cut is the argument.
- Layout: **Split Header/Footer** template fits the two-line rules scenes.
- Music: minimal — one percussive element, duck to −18 dB under narration.
