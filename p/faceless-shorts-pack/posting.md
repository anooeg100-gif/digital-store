# 30-Day Posting Plan (and Honest Expectations)

A realistic month for a faceless channel using this pack: **5 posts per week**, filmed
in batches, with two days a week left for review and rest. No bot schedules, no
"post 7× a day" burnout plan.

**The weekly rhythm this plan assumes:**
- **Sunday (or your day off):** batch-record 5 voiceovers in one sitting (~40 min), pick b-roll.
- **Monday–Friday:** edit + post one short/day (~30–45 min each at first, ~20 min once you have a preset).
- **Saturday:** 20 minutes — read every comment, reply to all, write down what the retention graph says.

## Honest expectations (read this on day 1, not day 30)

**What this plan actually produces:** a repeatable process, a library of 19 posts,
and real data about what your audience watches to the end. That is the deliverable.

**What it does not promise:**
- **No view guarantee.** Nothing about this format, this pack, or any pack guarantees
  views. A strong hook improves your odds; it does not set them. Accounts with the
  same script, same niche, and same posting count get wildly different results —
  that variance is real, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling certainty
  they don't have.
- **No income claims.** This document contains no earnings figures because you
  shouldn't trust any that come with a digital product. Money from a faceless channel
  arrives through trust built over time — sponsorships, your own product, affiliate
  links, clients — and every one of those needs an audience that already believes you.
- **No "algorithm hack."** Retention comes from clarity and pacing, not from hashtags,
  posting hour, or video length tricks. Post at a consistent time and stop optimizing it.

**What typically happens, described without numbers:**
- The first 10–20 posts behave like market research. Many will underperform regardless
  of quality. Read them as data on hooks, not as verdicts on you.
- Growth is lumpy, not linear. A month of flat output can precede a single post that
  changes the channel — you only have the option to keep posting, not to schedule the
  breakthrough.
- Comments are more useful than views at this stage. Five real questions from humans
  tell you what to make next; five thousand passive views don't.
- The people who make it to day 90 are mostly the ones who built a batch habit and
  didn't renegotiate it every morning.

**Guardrails:**
- Never buy followers, views, or engagement pods — they poison your analytics, which
  are the only honest feedback you have.
- Cite sources for any claim with a number in it; correct errors in the comments
  instead of deleting them.
- Follow each platform's monetization and spam rules; undisclosed ads get flagged.
- Cap the work: 45 minutes/day editing, 20 minutes/day engaging. Past that, quality
  drops and resentment starts — both are visible on screen.
